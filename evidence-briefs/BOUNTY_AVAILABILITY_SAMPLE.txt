HOW TO USE THIS BRIEF

Separate four evidence layers:
- advertised reward;
- current upstream work availability;
- sponsor acceptance of a particular deliverable;
- verified settled, withdrawable payment to the intended recipient.

For a claimable task, write down the exact remaining scope and acceptance check
before implementation. Recheck redirects, current state, competing work and
payout conditions. Preserve unknowns instead of treating them as positive facts.
