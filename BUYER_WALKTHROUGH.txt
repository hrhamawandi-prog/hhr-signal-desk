TRY IT

WHAT CHANGED
The baseline has three rows: linen-shirt in sizes S and M, plus coffee-mug.
The proposed file has two linen-shirt rows.

Finding                         Record   What to decide
BLANK_PRODUCT_FIELD             2        Vendor went from Nexus Sample to blank.
OPTIONS_CHANGED                 2        Size S became Small; the SKU stayed LN-S.
PRICE_CHANGED                   2        Price changed from 29.00 to 25.00.
BLANK_PRICE                     3        Size M's previously supplied price is blank.
HANDLE_NOT_IN_FILE              none     coffee-mug is absent from the proposed file.

A blank field and an omitted column are different situations. Review the actual
overwrite setting and intended values before an import. The tool flags the
supplied blank Vendor and Price cells; it does not choose your business values.

A matching SKU does not make an option change harmless. Investigate whether your
store's integrations depend on variant IDs before changing option values.

The omitted mug is an informational note. Its absence from this update file does
not establish that it should be deleted. This comparison does not delete anything.

WHO THIS FITS
A merchant or catalog operator preparing recurring CSV updates who wants an
offline comparison of supported fields. It requires no store credentials or
network upload. Limits: 10 MB and 25,000 rows per file.

WHO SHOULD USE SOMETHING ELSE
Anyone needing a full import validator, live store inspection, inventory-location
reconciliation, metafield validation, automatic fixes or a Shopify importer.
No flags is not a guarantee that an import is safe. Keep a fresh export and
validate a small update through your normal workflow.
